Foreword

About Tera Term Mac

Tera Term Mac is a port of Tera Term to macOS. Its main features have been rewritten in Swift / AppKit, and the TTL macro engine and the file transfer protocols are ported from Tera Term's own source code.

Tera Term Mac is the Mac edition of Tera Term, approved by its original author, T. Teranishi. It is developed by a separate team from TeraTerm Project, which develops the Windows version. Please send bug reports and requests to Tera Term Mac.

About Tera Term

Tera Term is a terminal emulator created in 1994 by Takashi Teranishi. Since 2004 it has been developed by the TeraTerm Project and published as open source software under the BSD license. Tera Term Mac is based on this Tera Term.

About Tera Term for Macintosh (Ver 1.6)

There once was a Tera Term Mac (Ver 1.6) for the Macintosh. Its author is T. Teranishi. This app (Tera Term Mac) is a separate, new port based on today's Tera Term.
